## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze business profitability and present key insights through an interactive and visually engaging Excel dashboard.The dashboard helps users quickly understand which customers, products, categories, and regions contribute most to overall profit


## Data Set
<a href = "https://github.com/Akshayparimella/Excel-Interactive-Executive-Dashboard/blob/main/Excel_Dashboard_Project.xlsx">Data Set</a>


## Key Features
📈 Profit Analysis by Customer Segment

Analyzes profitability across different customer segments to identify the most profitable segments.

📦 Profit Analysis by Product Category

Compares profit performance across different product categories.

🌎 Profit Analysis by Region

Provides a regional view of profitability to identify stronger and weaker-performing regions.

🛍️ Profit by Product Container

Analyzes profit based on different product container types.

📊 Profit by Product Sub-Category

Provides a detailed comparison of profitability across product sub-categories.

🔎 Profit by Product

Analyzes individual products to identify products contributing to overall profitability.

## Dashboard Visualizations
The dashboard includes:
Bar Chart – Profit by Customer Segment
Bar Chart – Profit by Product Category
Doughnut Chart – Profit by Region
Pie Chart – Profit by Product Container
Line Chart – Profit by Product Sub-Category
Line Chart – Profit by Product

  
## Business Insights
This dashboard can be used to:
Identify the most profitable customer segments
Compare profitability across product categories
Analyze regional profit performance
Identify profitable product containers
Compare product sub-category performance
Identify individual products contributing to profitability
Support data-driven business decisions


## Project Outcome
The project converts business data into an easy-to-understand visual dashboard, allowing users to analyze profitability from multiple business dimensions and quickly identify important performance trends.
